### Option 2: LinkedIn Post
Can graph databases and agentic AI eliminate fraud analyst burnout?

For the TigerGraph × Hacker House Goa Hackathon, our team built an Autonomous Agentic Fraud Investigation System using TigerGraph Savanna and LangGraph.

Instead of treating machine learning risk scores as ground truth, our agent investigates transactions like an expert Tier-3 fraud analyst:
1️⃣ Deep Graph Traversal: Executes 5 specialized GSQL queries directly in TigerGraph to uncover hidden multi-card rings, shared device fingerprints, and billing region anomalies.
2️⃣ GraphRAG & Case Memory: Retrieves historical closed investigations and regulatory policy clauses stored as embeddings in the graph.
3️⃣ Adaptive Uncertainty Loops: Requests simulated step-up authentication when evidence is ambiguous, capturing Next-Best-Action (NBA) evolution before and after investigation.
4️⃣ Regulatory Compliance: Automatically drafts SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) narratives following FinCEN standards.

We ran the agent across the 20 official benchmark cases, achieving 100% automated completion with full explainability audit trails.
